# 13: Wide sibling rows: a parent with many children doesn't fit a page

**Status:** ready-for-agent

**Blocked by:** 10, 09

**Category:** enhancement

**Slice:** bike-radar-model slice 1 (commit `b6a3056`), view `systemRequirements`. Split out of [10](10-wide-trees.md) during triage.

## Seen

`systemSpec`'s 11 nested requirements sit in one row, giving a 5618 px wide picture. At page width the text is too small to read.

## Comments

> *This was generated by AI during triage.*

### Triage notes

The layered layout puts all children of one parent in one layer. Only disconnected groups wrap (900 px). The Standard Notation's parts trees (slides 35–37) keep children in one row and never show a tree too wide for it, so the notation doesn't decide this. Grilled below.

### Grilling decisions

1. **Automatic:** when a parent's children would make a row wider than the page-width limit the layout already uses for disconnected groups (900 px), the layout stacks them without user action. Hand-moved nodes in the Layout still win.
2. **Only leaves stack:** children with no children of their own drawn beneath them are stacked vertically, in Model order (org-chart compact form).
3. **Edges:** the diamond stays at the parent's bottom centre (ticket 10). The trunk runs straight down from it, the leaves sit just right of the trunk, and each has a short horizontal branch into its left edge.
4. **Mixed parents:** leaves stack on the left, and children with their own subtrees stay in a row to the right, on the same trunk. Model order holds within each group, not between them.
5. **Still too wide:** a row of subtrees that is still too wide after stacking is accepted. Subtrees don't wrap.
6. **Scope:** General Diagrams only. No per-parent option in the Layout.

## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** When a parent's children would make a General Diagram wider than a page, the leaves among them are stacked vertically beside the parent's composition trunk.

**Current behavior:**
All children of one parent are placed side by side in one layer of the layered layout, however many there are. Only disconnected groups of nodes wrap onto a new row past the page-width limit. `systemSpec`'s 11 nested requirements make a 5618 px wide picture.

**Desired behavior:**
- **When:** for each parent in a General Diagram (tree view or Tool-defined) whose children, side by side, would be wider than the page-width limit the layout already uses for wrapping disconnected groups.
- **What stacks:** its **leaf children** (those with no children of their own drawn beneath them) are placed in a single column, in Model order, below the parent.
- **Edges:** the composition diamond stays at the parent's bottom centre. The trunk runs straight down from it past the column, and each leaf sits just right of the trunk with a short horizontal branch into its left edge.
- **Mixed parents:** children with their own subtrees stay side by side, to the right of the column, hanging from the same trunk as in ticket 10.
- **Narrow rows:** a parent whose children fit within the limit is laid out exactly as with ticket 10 alone.
- **Hand-moved nodes:** a child with a position in the Layout keeps it. The rest of its siblings are laid out as above.
- **Recursion:** this applies at every level, so a subtree's own leaves stack too.

**Key interfaces:**
- The layered layout for General Diagrams. Today it takes node sizes and edges and wraps only disconnected groups. It needs to know each parent's composition children and their order to place a stack.
- The composition tree drawing from ticket 10, extended to branches that enter a child from the left.
- The page-width constant already used for wrapping.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A test: a parent with 11 leaf children whose row would exceed the limit places them in one column, in Model order, and the diagram is no wider than the limit (given each child fits).
- [ ] In that test, one diamond sits at the parent's bottom centre. Every edge segment is horizontal or vertical, and each branch ends at its child's left edge.
- [ ] A test: a parent with 3 narrow leaf children (fitting the limit) still places them in a row.
- [ ] A test: a parent with 6 leaves and 2 children with their own subtrees, too wide in one row: the leaves stack on the left, and the 2 subtrees sit side by side to the right.
- [ ] A child placed by hand in the Layout keeps its position.
- [ ] bike-radar-model `systemRequirements` (commit `b6a3056`) exports no wider than the limit plus one requirement box.

**Out of scope:**
- Wrapping rows of subtrees.
- A per-parent row/stack option in the Layout.
- Interconnection Diagrams.
