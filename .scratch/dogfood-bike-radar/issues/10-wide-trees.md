## Agent Brief

**Category:** enhancement
**Summary:** In General Diagrams, a parent's composition edges are drawn as one tree: one diamond at the parent, a trunk, a bar, and orthogonal branches to each child, as in the Standard Notation's parts trees.

**Current behavior:**
Each composition edge (a usage under its owner, or a definition to the type of a part it composes) is a separate straight line from the parent's border toward the child's centre, with its own filled diamond at the parent. With several children, the diamonds pile up at one point on the parent's border. That point is often the side rather than the bottom. The lines fan out diagonally and can cross the parent's box.

**Desired behavior:**
- **One tree per parent:** a parent's composition edges to children placed below it are drawn together as one tree:
  - one filled diamond at the middle of the parent's bottom edge;
  - a vertical trunk down to a horizontal bar spanning the children;
  - a vertical branch from the bar to the top-middle of each child.
- **Edge labels:** a definition-level composition still shows its labels (the usage name and multiplicity) near the child end of its branch.
- **One child:** the tree reduces to a straight vertical line with the diamond at the parent.
- **Not below the parent:** a child the layout or the user puts beside or above its parent keeps today's single-edge drawing. Leaves stacked by [13](13-wide-sibling-rows.md) are not an exception: they hang from the same trunk with a horizontal branch each, so the trunk/branch drawing must allow a branch to enter a child's left edge as well as its top.
- **User bends:** an edge with user-set bend points in the Layout keeps them, and is drawn as its own edge, not part of the tree.
- The same applies to requirement trees (nested requirements under a composite requirement) and part trees alike. Other edge kinds are unchanged.

**Key interfaces:**
- General Diagram edge routing and its composition notation (the diamond), currently one route per edge.
- Scene items for edges: the tree needs a shared trunk and bar that belong to no single edge. Hit-testing and selection of an individual composition edge in the GUI should still work, by its branch.
- Golden SVGs for General Diagrams with composition, which will change.

**Acceptance criteria:**
- [ ] A test: a parent with three children below it draws exactly one composition diamond, at the parent's bottom-centre.
- [ ] In that test, every segment of the tree is horizontal or vertical, and each child's branch ends at its top-middle.
- [ ] A definition-level composition keeps its usage-name and multiplicity label beside its branch.
- [ ] A composition edge with user bend points in the Layout is drawn as before.
- [ ] bike-radar-model `systemRequirements` (commit `b6a3056`): one diamond under `systemSpec`, and no edge crosses its box.
- [ ] Selecting a child's branch in the GUI selects that composition edge.

**Out of scope:**
- Wrapping wide rows: [13](13-wide-sibling-rows.md).
- Child order: [09](09-tree-children-in-model-order.md).
- Specialization, typing, satisfy and connection edges.
- Interconnection Diagrams.
